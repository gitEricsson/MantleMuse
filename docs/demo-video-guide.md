# MantleMuse: Demo Video Guide & Script

This document provides a structured walkthrough and script for the MantleMuse demo video. The goal is to showcase the protocol's ability to fractionalize cultural assets (Art & Music) on the Mantle Network using USDT.

---

## 🎬 Video Structure Overview
1. **The Hook** (Home Page Intro)
2. **Onboarding** (Wallet Connect & Faucet)
3. **The Marketplace** (Explore & Filtering)
4. **Investment Flow** (Asset Detail & Multi-step Transaction)
5. **Portfolio Management** (Yield Tracking & Claiming)
6. **Admin God Mode** (Asset Creation & Payout Distribution)
7. **Closing** (The Vision)

---

## 📽️ Scene-by-Scene Guide

### Scene 1: The Landing Page (The "Wow" Factor)
*   **Navigation**: `http://localhost:3008/`
*   **Visual**: Scroll through the hero section, the high-fidelity stats (TVL, APY), and the "Why Invest" section.
*   **Script**: 
    > "Welcome to MantleMuse. We are bringing the $1.7 Trillion alternative asset market to the Mantle Network. MantleMuse allows anyone, anywhere, to own a piece of blue-chip art or world-class music royalties for as little as 10 USDT. By leveraging Mantle's hyper-scale performance, we make cultural investment liquid, transparent, and high-yield."

### Scene 2: Seamless Onboarding
*   **Navigation**: Click "Connect Wallet" in the Navbar.
*   **Action**: Use the Faucet button to get 10k USDT.
*   **Script**:
    > "Starting is simple. No sign-up forms—just connect your wallet. For this demo, we've integrated a gasless faucet on the Mantle Sepolia Testnet. With one click, we get our starting capital of 10,000 USDT to begin building our cultural portfolio."

### Scene 3: The Marketplace (Explore)
*   **Navigation**: `http://localhost:3008/explore`
*   **Action**: Use the "Art" or "Music" filters. Hover over cards to show animations.
*   **Script**:
    > "Our Marketplace is where the magic happens. Here, you'll find curated opportunities—from Basquiat masterpieces to streaming-heavy music catalogs. Each asset is fractionalized into thousands of yield-bearing USDT tokens. You can filter by asset type, risk profile, or return focus to find exactly what fits your strategy."

### Scene 4: The Investment (Deep Dive)
*   **Navigation**: Click on an asset (e.g., "Basquiat: Warrior").
*   **Action**: Enter an amount (e.g., 500 USDT), click "Invest Now", and go through the multi-step approval flow.
*   **Script**:
    > "Let’s look at this Basquiat piece. We can see the projected annual return and the share price. Let’s invest 500 USDT. Notice the smooth, multi-step transaction process. First, we approve the USDT usage, then we finalize the purchase. This is all happening on the Mantle blockchain, ensuring immutable ownership of our fractions."

### Scene 5: Portfolio & Yield
*   **Navigation**: `http://localhost:3008/portfolio`
*   **Action**: Show the holdings table. Highlight the "Unclaimed Yield" incrementing.
*   **Script**:
    > "Now, our dashboard. This is our Command Center. We can track our total value across different assets. But here’s the best part: Real-time Yield. As these assets generate revenue in the real world, the data is pushed on-chain. We can see our USDT rewards accumulating in real-time and claim them with a single click directly into our wallet."

### Scene 6: Admin Dashboard (Demo Utility)
*   **Navigation**: `http://localhost:3008/admin` -> "Add New Asset" or "Distribute Yield".
*   **Action**: Show the form for creating a new asset or distributing USDT revenue.
*   **Script**:
    > "For the judges, we've built a 'God Mode' Admin Dashboard. Here, we can mint new Real World Assets on-chain in seconds. We can also simulate real-world success by distributing revenue. When we deposit USDT here, every shareholder on the platform immediately sees their pending yield increase, showcasing our automated distribution engine."

### Scene 7: Wrap Up
*   **Navigation**: Back to Home page.
*   **Script**:
    > "MantleMuse isn't just a marketplace; it's a bridge. We're bridging the gap between high-value cultural assets and the liquidity of DeFi. Powered by Mantle, scaled with USDT, and designed for the next generation of investors. Join us in tokenizing the world's culture."

## 💡 Recording Tips
1.  **Dark Mode**: Keep the app in its native dark-premium theme for the best aesthetic.
2.  **Toasts**: Make sure the browser's "Success" toasts are visible in the corner when transactions complete.
3.  **Mantle Explorer**: If you have time, briefly show a transaction hash on the Mantle Sepolia Explorer to prove the "RealFi" nature of the app.
4.  **Cursor**: Use a highlight cursor if possible to make following your clicks easier.
